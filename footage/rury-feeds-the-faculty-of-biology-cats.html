Rury Feeds the Faculty of Biology Cats

One of the most important aspects of The Cats of the Faculty of Biology was Rury feeding them. Although we showed the students feeding them, as well, his relationship with these cats was the central point of the documentary. The footage here comprises the entirety of their afternoon feeding on a Sunday, as he came in (as he usually does) to feed them even on weekends.
